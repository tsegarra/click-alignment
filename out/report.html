Whisper → MFA vs. manual clicks

Summary

Error = MFA time − click time; positive means MFA is later than the click. “After bias removed” subtracts the mean error of that row, measured on the same syllables. “Vowel onset, minus its mean bias” is a reference for VPR: MFA’s vowel onset moved earlier by a constant, where each syllable’s shift is the mean vowel error of all the other scored syllables.

VPR vs. MFA vowel onset

VPR takes the halfway point of the steepest 10 ms loudness jump before the vowel when that jump is at least 15 dB (a burst), and otherwise MFA’s vowel onset moved 21 ms earlier. Each syllable’s |err| under VPR is compared with its |err| under the baseline. Changes within ±5 ms count as unchanged (MFA works in 10 ms frames). “Change” columns are VPR − baseline, so negative means VPR is closer to the click. The 21 ms was measured on these same clicks, so the shifted rows here are in-sample; the holdout data is the real test.
